Hygeon (n.) /ˈhaɪ-dʒiː-ən/

Possibly derived from a spontaneous collision between hy(drant) and (pi)geon.

(pi)geon

我是纽约城市里的一只鸽子。
居无定所
大部分时候随地落脚，总能成功躲避人群车辆 — 喝水，散步，扑腾
在空中织布，不停歇

I am a pigeon in NYC,
without a fixed dwelling.
Most of the time I settle wherever I please—
Quivering, wandering,
weaving through the sky
restlessly.

羽毛使我轻盈，直觉领先思索，
从公园花丛栅栏，到车边水潭；
我啄着落下的贝果碎屑，凌乱的自由。
我不知道接下来我该去哪里，
所以我让喧嚣带着我走，
风暴比安静更像活着。

Feathers make me light, instinct outruns my mind.
The gutter's thin trickle of rain,
the warm exhaust of a passing train,
the falling bagel crumbs — scattered pieces of freedom.
I never know where I am headed next,
so I let the wind carry me forward.
Storms make me feel more alive.

10月13日，晴

我落在她的头顶
她好像有点冷漠
其实我很羡慕她
不论多大的疾风她都不怕
如果今天我可以做一个消防栓
好像也不错。

Today I landed on her head.
The metal is cold.
I envy her toughness.
She stands no matter how severe the wind.
It might be nice
to be a hydrant today.

hy(drant)

我是纽约城市里的一个消防栓。
54街莱克星顿大道
最繁华的地方，永不停息的人流
我只是站在这里
平静得像一片荒原上的湖，纹丝不动

I am a hydrant in NYC.
54th Street, Lexington Avenue.
A hectic city, a sleepless crowd.
I only stand here—
calm as a lake in the middle of a wasteland,
utterly unmoving.

金属教人沉默，
喝完咖啡的人随手给我换上新帽子，
我学会喜欢上每一顶帽子。
我不知道风会带我去哪里，
所以我宁愿不动得扎在地底。
稳定比冒险来的简单。

Metal teaches silence.
Someone leaves me a new Starbucks hat in passing.
I learn to like every hat.
I don't know where the wind might take me,
so I choose to root myself in the ground.
Adventure is not my instinct.

10月13日，晴

她落在我的头顶，
这顶帽子很暖和。
其实我很羡慕她 —
不论风带她去哪她都不怕
如果今天我可以做一只鸽子，
好像也不错。

Today she landed on my head.
This hat is warmer.
I envy her for her feathers.
She flies wherever the wind takes her.
It might be nice
to be a pigeon today.

我们没有交换羽毛和金属，
我还是我她还是她；
只是在这条街道的狭小角落，
在那片没有波纹的湖，
和永不消停的天空之间
相遇了。

We didn't switch skins.
I'm still a hydrant, she is still a pigeon.
I'm still a pigeon, she is still a hydrant.
We simply met
in a narrow corner of the street
between a lake that never moved
and a sky that never rested.
